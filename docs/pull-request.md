The self-assessment now offers an optional front-camera capture or local JPG/PNG/WebP photo. It reuses one unchanged photo beside six sets of labelled colour drapes, with consistent positioning and zoom. Desktop shows simultaneous comparisons; mobile provides labelled sample switches. Preview controls never select answers or change scoring, and the original photo-free questionnaire remains available.

Photos are processed in browser memory, resized locally and never sent to a server or stored in browser storage. Camera tracks stop on capture, cancellation, hidden page and unmount; late permission responses are stopped too. Remove, restart, refresh and navigation discard the local image. Security headers allow same-origin camera permission and local blob imagery while continuing to block microphone access.

Validation includes lint, strict TypeScript, unit/component lifecycle and uncertainty tests, production export, three-engine Playwright photo journeys and the existing site suite. Physical handset cameras and manual screen-reader review remain unverified; digital comparisons are illustrative styling guidance, not calibrated colour measurement or automatic facial analysis. Cross-engine CI and deployment evidence are recorded in PROGRESS.md and docs/verification.md.
